# Logo generation prompt

Tool: built-in `imagegen`. One original concept, followed by manually authored production SVG geometry.

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one polished brand identity presentation for ContextGate, a self-hosted Semantic Data Gateway for AI Agents.
Design a distinctive, exceptionally simple flat vector-style symbol: a dark deep-teal open hexagonal C-shaped gateway, its right side open; a short brighter teal horizontal path passes through the opening into a single circular semantic node at the center. The gateway has confident thick geometric strokes with gently softened corners, visually balanced at favicon size. A C and the inner gate/path suggest C/G without forcing literal typography. Meaning: business context passing through a controlled entrance. No shield, padlock, database cylinder, brain, sparkles, gradients, 3D, shadows or dense network diagram.
Color palette: deep teal #183F43, signal teal #32B6A0, warm white #F7FAF8.
Wide landscape brand presentation: generous warm-white background, large symbol to the left of a clean modern humanist sans-serif wordmark reading exactly "ContextGate", dark teal, semibold, excellent kerning. Under wordmark, smaller text reading exactly "Semantic Data Gateway for AI Agents". Keep the composition focused on this one identity, with an understated small monochrome version of the same symbol near the lower right. No mockups or other text. Create a professional actual usable flat logo design, with generous margins.
